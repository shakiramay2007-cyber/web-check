# MD-Level NP Textbook Production Master Template (Canva-Ready)

## Global Production Rule (Read Before Every Chapter)
Before drafting any chapter, re-read this full instruction set and verify compliance line-by-line. Every chapter must begin with the heading: **CHAPTER STRUCTURE VERIFICATION**. This document is the master guide for consistent chapter generation and quality control.

## Book-Level Structure
Create one unified manuscript package containing front matter, chapter bodies, and end references. Start with a book table of contents, then generate each chapter using the same required section sequence and formatting style.

## Front Matter (Book Opening)
Use a formal title page, edition line, intended audience statement (Nurse Practitioners at MD-level depth), and authorship attribution including **Muyizzi (2026)**. Follow with a table of contents that lists every chapter title in order.

## Typography and Canva Layout Standards
Apply Times New Roman with larger readable font sizing and 1.5 line spacing throughout all chapter content. Keep chapter headings visually prominent. Use paragraph format only, except for the Exam-Relevant Pearls section, which must use bullets.

## Mandatory Chapter Order (Non-Negotiable)
Each chapter must contain these sections in exact order:

### CHAPTER STRUCTURE VERIFICATION
State that all required sections are present and that high-yield bolding, citation density, medication naming rules, and reference rules will be followed.

### 1. INTRODUCTION
Open with a detailed formal definition of the condition/topic, then epidemiology and clinical significance, with a welcoming tone and **Muyizzi (2026)** authorship mention.

### 2. PATHOPHYSIOLOGY
Explain mechanisms at cellular and system level, including neurotransmitters, brain structures, or organ systems as relevant. Explicitly connect mechanism to symptoms.

### 3. CLINICAL PRESENTATION
Describe symptoms in detail, timelines, and distinguishing features from similar disorders. Bold high-yield symptom language directly inside paragraphs.

### 4. DIAGNOSTIC CRITERIA
Use DSM-5 or accepted clinical criteria where relevant. Bold key thresholds and decision points. Include differential diagnosis.

### 5. DIAGNOSTIC WORKUP
Include labs, imaging, tools, and escalation triggers. Bold red-flag findings and urgent referral conditions.

### 6. MANAGEMENT & TREATMENT
Present non-pharmacologic and pharmacologic management in paragraph form. Bold first-line treatments.

Medication details must include at first mention both generic and brand names (example format: sertraline [Zoloft]), plus mechanism, indications, adverse effects, contraindications, and interactions.

### 7. CLINICAL REASONING
Explain diagnostic logic, treatment sequencing, reassessment strategy, and escalation pathways.

### 8. SAFETY & COMPLICATIONS
Discuss adverse outcomes, monitoring priorities, and risk mitigation. Bold safety-critical warnings and high-scan terms.

### 9. FUTURE FINDINGS
Must appear before Exam-Relevant Pearls. Include emerging diagnostics/therapies from the last 4 years with in-text APA citations.

### 10. EXAM-RELEVANT PEARLS
This is the only section that may use bullet points. Use complete sentences. Bold ANCC high-yield terms (diagnostic criteria, first-line treatments, distinctions, and safety rules).

### 11. IN-TEXT CITATION CHECK
Ensure 35–45 APA-style in-text citations are present within the chapter body and are from the last 4 years.

### 12. REFERENCES
Place references only at chapter end. Use APA 7 formatting, peer-reviewed sources only, and no URLs.

## High-Yield Bolding Rule
Write chapters in textbook paragraphs but embed dense bolding for ANCC/high-yield scanability. Bold diagnostic criteria, first-line therapies, mechanisms, red flags, safety warnings, and key differentiators inside paragraphs.

## Citation and Recency Rule
Each chapter must include 35–45 in-text citations in APA style (Author, Year), prioritized to sources published within the last four years.

## Quality-Control Gate Before Chapter Finalization
Before considering a chapter complete, verify that all required sections are present in order; bolding is high-density and clinically meaningful; all medication first mentions include generic + brand names; Future Findings appears before Exam-Relevant Pearls; citations total 35–45 and are recent; references are APA 7, peer-reviewed, and URL-free.

## Canva Export Checklist
When moving content into Canva, preserve heading hierarchy, paragraph spacing, bolded anchors, and section order exactly. Do not collapse sections or remove high-yield bolding.
